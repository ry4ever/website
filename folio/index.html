Questions and answers

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing site data removes it, so export a backup from Settings → Data & storage.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start writing. Your name and workspace name are just local preferences.

Can I move my notes in or out?

Yes. Import Markdown files, paste Markdown straight into a page, and export any page as Markdown or HTML. The whole workspace exports as JSON.

Does it work on my phone?

The layout adapts to small screens: the sidebar slides in from the menu button and pages reflow to fit.
